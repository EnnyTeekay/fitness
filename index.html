FAQs

Is SweatsApp free?

Yes, there will be a free plan with gym search, coach booking, workout tracking and beginner programmes. Premium unlocks extras like full meal plans and the full video library.

I’m a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. SweatsApp is built for people starting or restarting. Your plan starts where you are, not where an athlete is.

Can I train from home?

Yes. Choose home workouts with no equipment, a mat and bands, or dumbbells.

Are the coaches qualified?

Every coach’s certification is checked by our team before they get a verified badge.

When are you launching?

We launch in Lagos first, then Abuja. Waitlist members get access first.

What do you do with my data?

We only use it to tell you about SweatsApp’s launch. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and never sell your data.
