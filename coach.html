FAQs

How much does it cost to join?

Joining is free. SweatsApp takes a commission on sessions and programmes booked through the platform. We’ll share exact rates before you go live.

When do I get paid?

Clients pay in-app. Your earnings, minus SweatsApp’s commission, are paid out weekly.

Can I bring my existing clients?

Yes. Invite them to SweatsApp and manage everyone in one dashboard.

I don’t have an international certification. Can I still join?

Yes. Upload what you have and our team will review it. Unverified coaches can still list, but verified coaches rank higher.

Do I have to coach in person?

No. You can coach in person, online, or both.

What do you do with my data?

We only use it to tell you about SweatsApp’s launch. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and never sell your data.
