FAQs

How much does SweatsApp cost for gyms?

Plans start from ₦8,000 a month. There’s no long-term contract, and you can change plans any time.

Do you charge for new members you send us?

No. Being listed and discovered on SweatsApp has no per-member fee.

Do I need special equipment for check-in?

No. A phone or tablet at reception can scan member QR codes. NFC readers are optional.

What if our internet goes down?

Check-ins are saved on the device and sync automatically when you’re back online.

Can you move our existing members over?

Yes. Founding partners get free setup, including importing your member list.

Is our member data safe?

Yes. Data is encrypted, payments run through Paystack, and we comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Your data is never shared with other gyms.
